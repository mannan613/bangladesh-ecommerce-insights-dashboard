# 📊 Bangladesh E-Commerce Insights Dashboard

## 🧠 Project Overview

This project analyzes real-world style e-commerce data from a Bangladesh-based online retail business. The goal is to extract actionable business insights from order, customer, and product data to improve revenue, customer retention, and profitability.

This project demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics workflow using SQL, Tableau, and Excel/CSV datasets.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The e-commerce company wants to better understand:

* Which products and regions generate the most revenue and profit
* Customer purchasing behavior (new vs returning customers)
* Monthly sales trends and seasonality
* Product return patterns affecting profitability

The final goal is to support data-driven business decision-making.

---

## 📊 Key Analysis Areas

### 🛒 Sales Performance

* Total revenue trends over time
* Month-on-month growth analysis
* Peak sales period identification

### 📦 Product Analysis

* Top-performing products by revenue and quantity sold
* Underperforming product categories
* Product return rate analysis

### 👥 Customer Analysis

* New vs returning customer ratio
* Customer purchase behavior patterns
* Revenue contribution from repeat customers

### 🌍 Regional Analysis

* Sales distribution across regions in Bangladesh
* High-performing and low-performing regions

---

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

* A small percentage of products contribute to the majority of total revenue (Pareto Principle).
* Certain months show significant sales spikes, indicating strong seasonal demand.
* Returning customers generate higher lifetime value than new customers.
* Some product categories have elevated return rates, reducing profitability.
* Revenue is concentrated in a few major regions, creating opportunities for market expansion.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Focus marketing efforts on high-revenue regions.
* Introduce customer loyalty and retention programs.
* Optimize inventory management for top-performing products.
* Investigate and reduce high-return products.
* Leverage seasonal demand patterns for targeted campaigns.
